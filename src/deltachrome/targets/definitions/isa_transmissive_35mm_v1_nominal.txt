Two further steps sit between this file and a measurement, and both are choices rather than arithmetic: the published data is DENSITY ONLY, anchored here so the lightest step reads L* 96 -- FADGI's own transparency highlight aim -- because a density ladder carries no lightness of its own; and a* = b* = 0 restates the manufacturer's claim that the scales are spectrally neutral as an aim. Density describes the film; L* describes the appearance of a reproduction, and for a transmissive original the mapping between them IS the tone response being measured. Treat tone response against this file as advisory of a rendering intent, not as conformance.
