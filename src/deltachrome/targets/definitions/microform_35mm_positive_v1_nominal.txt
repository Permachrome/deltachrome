DELTACHROME_NOMINAL_CAVEAT	"Manufacturer nominal aim values for the model, not a measurement of any individual chart. Two further steps sit between this file and a measurement, and both are choices rather than arithmetic: the published data is DENSITY ONLY, anchored here so the lightest step reads L* 96 -- FADGI's own transparency highlight aim -- because a density ladder carries no lightness of its own; and a* = b* = 0 restates the manufacturer's claim that the scales are spectrally neutral as an aim. Density describes the film; L* describes the appearance of a reproduction, and for a transmissive original the mapping between them IS the tone response being measured. Treat tone response against this file as advisory of a rendering intent, not as conformance. The densities DESCEND with patch number on this positive, which is the published order and is kept. Patch1 is the densest step and sits at the far end of each L's short arm; Patch10 is the clear step at the far end of the long arm. The negative of the same chart numbers the same physical steps in the same direction and its densities ascend, which is what says the numbering follows the print rather than the polarity. The two files are NOT interchangeable. A positive and a negative of one chart are different specimens with different aim data, and running either against the other's file inverts the ramp."
