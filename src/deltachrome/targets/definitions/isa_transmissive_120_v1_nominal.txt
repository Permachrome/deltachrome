DELTACHROME_NOMINAL_CAVEAT	"Manufacturer nominal aim values for the model, not a measurement of any individual chart. Two further steps sit between this file and a measurement, and both are choices rather than arithmetic: the published data is DENSITY ONLY, anchored here so the lightest step reads L* 96 -- FADGI's own transparency highlight aim -- because a density ladder carries no lightness of its own; and a* = b* = 0 restates the manufacturer's claim that the scales are spectrally neutral as an aim. Density describes the film; L* describes the appearance of a reproduction, and for a transmissive original the mapping between them IS the tone response being measured. Treat tone response against this file as advisory of a rendering intent, not as conformance. CHOSEN PARTLY BY FIT, which is said rather than hidden: four captures of two formats from two sources agree on this ladder and disagree with the one published for the 120, but only ISA can confirm what the chart prints."
