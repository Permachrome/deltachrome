DELTACHROME_NOMINAL_CAVEAT	"Manufacturer nominal aim values for the model, not a measurement of any individual chart. Two further steps sit between this file and a measurement, and both are choices rather than arithmetic: the published data is DENSITY ONLY, anchored here so the lightest step reads L* 96 -- FADGI's own transparency highlight aim -- because a density ladder carries no lightness of its own; and a* = b* = 0 restates the manufacturer's claim that the scales are spectrally neutral as an aim. Density describes the film; L* describes the appearance of a reproduction, and for a transmissive original the mapping between them IS the tone response being measured. Treat tone response against this file as advisory of a rendering intent, not as conformance. These ten densities are the first ten of the twelve OpenDICE publishes for the ISA 120 target, to the digit. That is worth knowing and is not evidence the two charts are related: it is a reason to check both files before trusting either. The two files are NOT interchangeable. A positive and a negative of one chart are different specimens with different aim data, and running either against the other's file inverts the ramp."
