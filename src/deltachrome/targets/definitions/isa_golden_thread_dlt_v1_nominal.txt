DELTACHROME_NOMINAL_CAVEAT	"A batch mean of 50 individually measured units, not a measurement of the chart in front of you and not a design aim. It is the best available answer to 'what does a chart of this model look like' and it is still not an answer to 'what does THIS chart look like', which is why a run against it reports ADVISORY and makes no conformance claim for the specimen. Two things follow. First, if your own unit's serial is published -- and most are -- fetch it: Deltachrome reads the chart's OpenQualia label and will pair the capture with that unit's own file, which is a conformance test where this is not. Second, the values printed on your own card are a batch average too, of your batch rather than of the population; they are not more authoritative than these and are drawn from a smaller sample. The residual error this leaves is genuine unit-to-unit variation and cannot be reduced by averaging more units: held-out error converges to the floor set by the population's own scatter by about ten units."
